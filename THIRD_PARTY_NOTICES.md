## Persian terminology sources

The approved AFTA glossary supplied by the project and «فرهنگ لغات امنیت سایبر»، چاپ اول ۱۴۰۳, are independent terminology sources. They are not relicensed by this project. Complete JSON/Markdown uploads, full definitions, account-specific Library links and PDFs are excluded from the public archive. The importer preserves them locally for authorized use and provenance. Only original boolean-true supplementary entries become operational; held records remain excluded.
